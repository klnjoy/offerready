# "Keep Asking Why" — interactive

The single skill that separates a memorized answer from a senior one: **defending
a decision under escalating follow-ups.** This interactive drill plays the
interviewer. You pick a decision, face a chain of progressively harder "why?"
questions, answer each (out loud or typed), then reveal what a strong answer
covers — and decide whether to be pushed harder.

!!! tip "How to use it"
    Answer *before* you reveal. The reveal is the model, not the prompt. Your
    best depth per scenario is saved in this browser. Aim to reach the end of a
    chain without stalling — that's the interview-ready bar.

## Why this is the differentiator

Most prep teaches *what* something is. Interviews for senior, staff, principal,
and FDE roles test whether you can **defend the decision** — "why hybrid search?
why that weighting? prove it improved. latency doubled, now what? at 10× scale?"
This drill trains exactly that reflex.

It's the interactive companion to the written
[The Interviewer Keeps Asking Why](Interview_Why_Chains.md) — use that for the
full explanation and more chains, and this to practice under pressure.
