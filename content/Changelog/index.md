# Changelog

New features and improvements, newest first.

## October 2026

### Week of 13 October
- **Interview Copilot (practice).** Ask any interview question and get an
  answer outline built from your resume and stories. Every point is labelled
  with where it came from, and numbers you haven't shown it are flagged.
- **Retry and compare.** Answer again and see your score and each criterion
  change, for example "Vague → Solid".
- **Your answers library.** Save the best version of each answer for each job, and
  practise it again any time.
- **New pages:** role guides for [AI](../For/ai-engineers/index.md),
  [data](../For/data-engineers/index.md),
  [platform](../For/platform-engineers/index.md) and
  [staff](../For/staff-engineers/index.md) engineers, plus
  [About](../About/index.md), [Compare](../Compare/index.md) and
  [Security and your data](../Security/index.md).

### Week of 6 October
- **Simpler path to ready.** Each job now has three steps: job and gaps,
  practice, mock interview. None of the four practice tools is required.
- **Leave and come back.** Drills and practice save your progress. Use Save and
  exit or Finish now at any point, and switch between practice tools without losing anything.
- **Rubric feedback.** Each criterion is scored with a quote from your answer, and you get a
  "staff upgrade" tip.
- **Interview debrief and outcome check-in.** Write down each round; questions that went badly come back in your plan.
- **Easier job import.** Paste links from more job boards, get the exact
  job title, and follow a clear fit → tailor resume → re-check flow.
- **Practice tracks.** ML/AI platform and architect tracks, plus 44 new
  questions for voice and text interviews.
- **Plans and passes.** One-time passes and a mock pack, alongside monthly plans.
- **Your data.** Your data follows your account across devices, the app can be
  installed, and there are export and delete controls.
- **Voice mock interviews, story bank and the help assistant** launched.
