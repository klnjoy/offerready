# Changelog

New features and improvements, newest first.

## October 2026

### Week of 13 October
- **Keep up with AI.** A new page with news and free courses from Anthropic,
  OpenAI, Google DeepMind, LangChain and Hugging Face, the topics interviewers
  ask about (tokenization, agents, MCP, prompting) and a weekly habit that
  turns reading into a practised answer.
- **One Today, phone and computer.** The computer gets the same Today as the
  phone, in two columns. Job tabs are now **Today**, **Resume match** and
  **Interviews**; settings moved to **⚙︎** by the job name. On phones the tab
  row is gone.
- **Plans that fit a day.** Each day is about 40 minutes and starts with
  answering questions for the job. Missing experience becomes "Prepare your
  answer" instead of "Study", and readiness changes say what caused them.
- **A new Today on phones.** One **Start** button for today's session,
  readiness with its change this week, days to your interview, resume match,
  a week strip with your streak, and your gaps to close.
- **Tidier tabs.** Today's plan and Coach's context are grouped lists with
  one status each; Progress puts your top 3 (tap a row to start) above the
  score breakdown and asks for your interview date in one place.
- **No repeats across tabs.** Practice is one list of the five ways to
  practise, each with its own status (Next, progress, Done, weakest topic).
  More keeps only what isn't on another tab, and Today drops links that
  other tabs already have.
- **Skills this job wants.** On Resume match: which skills the job asks for,
  which your resume shows, and for each missing one how to learn it, a project
  to build, a resume line and an optional certification.
- **Phone layout.** A bottom bar (Today, Practice, Coach, Progress) and a **+**
  button for quick actions, one thumb-tap away.
- **Where to start** in the study library: pick how much time you have and
  your role, and read the pages in order. Also on the home page.
- **Who's interviewing you?** On Questions for this job, pick a recruiter,
  hiring manager, technical peer, director or business partner to see what
  they care about and get the questions they're likely to ask.
- **Will it get shortlisted?** The Resume match tab checks your resume for
  what screening software and recruiters look for, with a fix for each.
- **Faster.** Adding a job opens it as soon as it's saved and compares your
  resume in the background; questions for a job come in about half the time
  (16 first, then **Get more questions**).
- **Draft stories from my resume.** One click drafts up to 4 stories for the
  competencies a job needs that you have no story for, marked Draft until you
  check them.
- **Less typing.** **Draft it for me** turns a few words or a resume line
  into a full STAR story, and **Fill it in for me** turns your recap of an
  interview into a debrief with each question rated. Both leave anything they
  can't know for you to fill in.
- **Questions you were asked.** Every question from your real interviews, in
  one place across all your jobs, with your notes, how it went and how often
  it came up. Practise any of them with the AI Interview Coach. See
  [After the interview](../Help/after-the-interview.md#questions-you-were-asked).
- **Tailoring keeps your main resume.** Tailored bullets are now saved as a
  copy with that job, so your other jobs keep your main resume. See
  [Where the tailored copy is](../Help/jobs.md#where-the-tailored-copy-is).
- **Find your way like the study notes.** Every job page and practice tool
  shows a trail at the top (*Jobs › your job › step*), your jobs as folders
  on the left with their steps inside (in **Menu** on phones), "On this
  page" on the right, and previous / next links at the bottom. See [Getting around](../Help/jobs.md#getting-around).
- **Job settings.** A Settings tab on each job: title, company and interview
  date with **Save changes** and **Cancel**, your resume saved only when you
  choose **Save resume**, and deleting the job.
- **Complete Help Center.** Step-by-step instructions for every part of the
  app, with common questions on each page, a [full list of limits](../Help/account-billing.md),
  new pages on [your data and sync](../Help/your-data.md) and
  [getting the app](../Help/get-the-app.md), and a much longer
  [troubleshooting](../Help/troubleshooting.md) guide.
- **Architect guide.** A [role page](../For/architects/index.md) for solutions,
  AI, data and cloud architect interviews: discovery, trade-offs, governance and
  the case presentation.
- **Interview notes.** A plain notepad for during a real interview: type each
  question as it's asked, then turn your notes into a debrief in one click.
  No AI and no microphone.
- **Forward Deployed Engineer guide.** A [role page](../For/forward-deployed-engineers/index.md)
  for FDE loops: discovery, practical coding, customer scenarios and demos.
- **Help Center.** Step-by-step [help pages](../Help/index.md) for every part of the app.
- **AI Interview Coach.** Ask any interview question and get an
  answer outline built from your resume and stories. Every point is labelled
  with where it came from, and numbers you haven't shown it are flagged.
- **Retry and compare.** Answer again and see your score and each criterion
  change, for example "Vague → Solid".
- **Your answers library.** Save the best version of each answer for each job, and
  practise it again any time.
- **New pages:** role guides for [AI](../For/ai-engineers/index.md),
  [data](../For/data-engineers/index.md),
  [platform](../For/platform-engineers/index.md) and
  [staff](../For/staff-engineers/index.md) engineers, plus
  [About](../About/index.md), [Compare](../Compare/index.md) and
  [Security and your data](../Security/index.md).

### Week of 6 October
- **Simpler path to ready.** Each job now has three steps: job and gaps,
  practice, mock interview. None of the four practice tools is required.
- **Leave and come back.** Drills and practice save your progress. Use Save and
  exit or Finish now at any point, and switch between practice tools without losing anything.
- **Rubric feedback.** Each criterion is scored with a quote from your answer, and you get a
  "staff upgrade" tip.
- **Interview debrief and outcome check-in.** Write down each round; questions that went badly come back in your plan.
- **Easier job import.** Paste links from more job boards, get the exact
  job title, and follow a clear fit → tailor resume → re-check flow.
- **Practice tracks.** ML/AI platform and architect tracks, plus 44 new
  questions for voice and text interviews.
- **Plans and passes.** One-time passes and a mock pack, alongside monthly plans.
- **Your data.** Your data follows your account across devices, the app can be
  installed, and there are export and delete controls.
- **Voice mock interviews, story bank and the help assistant** launched.
