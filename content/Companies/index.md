# Company interview guides

How eight companies say they interview, taken from **their own careers pages and
official posts** (linked on every page), plus practice questions to prepare for it.

!!! info "What's here, and what isn't"
    - **The process and what they assess** come only from each company's published
      guidance. Every page lists its sources and the date we checked them.
    - **The practice questions are written by OfferReady** to practise what each company
      says it looks for. They are *not* leaked or reported questions from real interviews.
      Several companies ask candidates to keep their interview questions confidential,
      and we respect that.
    - OfferReady isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of these companies. Processes
      change by team, level and region: your recruiter's word always wins.

| Company | Their loop, in short | What they say they look for |
|---|---|---|
| [Amazon](amazon.md) | Phone screen, then 4–6 interviews incl. a Bar Raiser | The 16 Leadership Principles, STAR stories |
| [Google](google.md) | Structured interviews, same questions per role, rubric scoring | Role-related knowledge, problem solving, leadership |
| [Meta](meta.md) | Technical screen, then coding, design and behavioral | Approach, communication, design trade-offs, values |
| [Microsoft](microsoft.md) | Varies by role; recruiter explains the format | Growth mindset, customer obsession, STAR-R answers |
| [OpenAI](openai.md) | Intro calls, skills assessment, 4–6 hours of finals | Expertise or fast ramp-up, communication, mission fit |
| [Anthropic](anthropic.md) | Not published in detail | Its seven values; no AI help in live interviews |
| [Databricks](databricks.md) | Recruiter call, screen, 4–6 interviews, sometimes a presentation | Role competencies and six culture principles |
| [Snowflake](snowflake.md) | 30-min screen, technical rounds, 3–5 panel interviews | Coding, system design, behavioral, collaboration |
