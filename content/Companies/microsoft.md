## The process, as Microsoft describes it

Microsoft doesn't publish one fixed loop: after the first round, steps **vary by role**.
Interviews can include meeting teammates and cross-functional partners, coding exercises,
portfolio reviews or presenting work samples. **Your recruiter explains the format and
timeline.** Microsoft describes the interview as two-way: you're assessing the team too.

## What they look for

| Microsoft says | In practice |
|---|---|
| Growth mindset | Learning fast; applying feedback; connecting with others to push ideas |
| Diverse and inclusive | Seeking other perspectives; checking your own biases |
| One Microsoft | Working across teams; building on others' success |
| Customer obsession | Understanding customers and acting on it |
| Values | Respect, integrity, accountability |

## How to prepare (Microsoft's advice)

- Match your experience to the job description, with examples covering the situation,
  what you did, the outcome **and what you learned**.
- Answer behavioral questions with **STAR-R**: situation, task, action, result, **reflection**.
- Show your reasoning: ask clarifying questions, state assumptions, explain your approach.
  Microsoft says it doesn't expect you to know everything.
- Be ready to say how you've applied feedback and adapted your approach.
- Research the role, the industry and Microsoft's recent work; prepare questions to ask.

## Practice questions (written by OfferReady)

1. **Growth mindset:** Tell me about feedback that changed how you work. What did you do differently afterwards?
2. **Growth mindset:** Tell me about something you had to learn from scratch for a project. How did you learn it?
3. **One Microsoft:** Tell me about a time you needed another team's help and they had other priorities.
4. **Customer obsession:** Tell me about a decision you made from customer data rather than opinion.
5. **Reflection (STAR-R):** Tell me about a project you'd run differently today. What's the reflection?
6. **Technical:** Design an API for a to-do app used by teams. How do you handle offline edits and conflicts?
7. **Technical (AI/cloud):** How would you take an LLM feature from prototype to production on a cloud platform? What do you monitor?
