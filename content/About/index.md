# About OfferReady

OfferReady helps senior AI, data and platform engineers prepare for one
specific job interview and walk in able to defend real engineering decisions.

## Why it exists

General chatbots can write you a nice answer. What they can't do is tell you
whether *your* answer would hold up, keep track of what *this* job asks for, or
show you that you are actually improving. Senior interviews are lost on
follow-ups, on vague results and on trade-offs you can't defend. OfferReady is
built around exactly those three things.

## How it works

1. **One job at a time.** Add the job; see what it asks for and how your resume matches.
2. **Practise what that job will ask.** Questions, trade-off drills and STAR
   stories, graded against clear rubrics.
3. **Prove you got better.** Retry an answer and see the before-and-after.
4. **Rehearse the real thing.** Voice or text mock interviews with follow-ups.

## Our principles

- **Built from your real experience.** The Copilot and the resume tailoring
  never invent employers, projects or numbers. Every point is labelled with
  where it came from.
- **Practice only.** OfferReady is for preparing *before* an interview. We
  don't build hidden overlays or live answer feeds for real interviews, and our
  [Terms](../Terms/index.md) ask you not to use it that way.
- **Honest scores.** A score needs evidence quoted from your answer.
  Readiness is guidance, not a promise. See the
  [Disclaimer](../Disclaimer/index.md).
- **Your data stays yours.** You can export or delete it at any time. See
  [Privacy](../Privacy/index.md).
