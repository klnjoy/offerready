# Interview prep for Platform, DevOps and AI Platform Engineers

Platform interviews are about running other teams' workloads well: paved roads,
reliability, capacity and cost. For AI platform roles, they add model serving,
GPU capacity, an LLM gateway and shared evaluation.

## What the interviews test

| Round | What they listen for |
|---|---|
| **Infrastructure design** | Multi-tenant platforms, Kubernetes, networking, isolation, rollout strategy |
| **Reliability** | SLOs, error budgets, incident response, graceful degradation |
| **Performance and capacity** | Autoscaling, GPU utilisation, batching, latency budgets |
| **AI platform** | Model gateway, serving engines, eval and release gates, guardrails, audit |
| **Coding** | Automation in Python or Go, debugging, infrastructure as code |
| **Behavioral** | A platform you shipped to other teams and how you got them to adopt it |

## How to prepare with OfferReady

1. **Add the job.** The job page shows what that team runs and where your
   resume is thin. [Add a job →](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/analyze)
2. **Trade-off and incident drills.** Defend a rollout plan, then handle the
   follow-up: "the canary is fine, but p99 doubled for one tenant".
3. **AI Interview Coach.** Turn a real incident from your resume into a
   speakable answer, with each point labelled by where it came from.
4. **Mock interview.** Practise out loud with voice, then go over your weakest
   criteria.
