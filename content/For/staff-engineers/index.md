# Interview prep for Staff and Principal Engineers

At staff level, a correct answer is not enough. Interviewers want to hear
scope, ambiguity handled well, trade-offs explained in business terms, and
influence across teams without authority.

## What changes from senior

| Senior answer | Staff / principal answer |
|---|---|
| "I'd use Kafka for the queue." | "Kafka, because we need replay for the backfill; here is the operational cost and when I'd revisit it." |
| Solves the problem as asked | Questions the problem, sets requirements and names what is out of scope |
| One team's system | Org-wide impact: migration path, adoption, ownership |
| "We improved latency." | "p95 went from 900 ms to 250 ms, which unblocked the mobile launch." |

## How to prepare with OfferReady

1. **Rubric feedback shows the next level.** Every graded answer comes with a
   "staff upgrade": the one thing that would lift it from senior to staff.
2. **Trade-off drills.** The interviewer keeps asking "why?" until your
   reasoning holds or breaks. Practise until it holds.
3. **Leadership stories.** Prepare stories about influence, disagreement and
   failure, then let Answer Studio shape them into speakable answers without
   inventing anything.
4. **Retry and compare.** Answer, improve and answer again, and watch the
   criteria move from "Vague" to "Solid".
