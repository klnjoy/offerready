# Interview prep for Forward Deployed Engineers

FDE loops test whether you can take a vague customer problem, ship working
software inside the customer's environment, and explain it to people who don't
write code. The interviewer is judging two things at once: the engineering, and
whether a customer would trust you in the room.

## What the interviews test

| Round | What they listen for |
|---|---|
| **Decomposition and discovery** | You ask about users, constraints and success metrics before solving, then propose a clear first slice |
| **Practical coding** | Working code fast against a real API or messy data, with errors, retries and logging handled, talking as you go |
| **Customer scenario design** | An architecture that fits the customer's existing stack: data access, auth, evaluation, rollout and cost |
| **Live troubleshooting** | Calm, systematic debugging of something broken in production at a customer site |
| **Presentation or demo** | A clear story at the right level, honest about limits, with hostile questions handled well |
| **Behavioral** | Shipping under ambiguity, changing a customer's direction, feeding field learning back to the product team |

## What changes at staff level

A senior answer solves the problem as asked. A staff answer reframes it ("the
real issue is the scheduling data, not the model"), sequences the work in
phases, agrees acceptance criteria with the customer, and says what would kill
the project.

## How to prepare with OfferReady

1. **Add the job.** FDE postings vary a lot (model providers, AI startups,
   enterprise vendors). The job page shows what this team actually needs and
   where your resume is thin. [Add a job →](https://getofferready.app/analyze)
2. **Customer scenario drills.** In trade-off drills you make a design call
   for a customer, then hold it while the interviewer pushes on their stack,
   data access, cost and what happens when the model is wrong.
3. **Stories for the customer rounds.** Write STAR stories about ambiguous
   engagements, difficult stakeholders and a deployment you rescued. The
   [AI Interview Coach](https://getofferready.app/coach) shapes
   them into answers without inventing anything.
4. **Mock the presentation.** A voice mock with Deep mode keeps asking "why?",
   like a sceptical customer stakeholder.
5. **After each round,** use the interview notes pad and the debrief, so the
   questions that went badly come back in your plan.
