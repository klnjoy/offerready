# Interview prep for Architects

Architect interviews are less about knowing a service and more about judgment:
turning a vague business need into a design, choosing between options with
real costs, and getting people to agree on it. Expect most of the loop to be
open-ended, with the interviewer changing a constraint halfway through.

This guide covers **solutions, AI, data and cloud architect** roles, from
senior to principal and enterprise level.

## What the interviews test

| Round | What they listen for |
|---|---|
| **Architecture design** | Requirements first, then a design you can draw in layers, with the risks named before you're asked |
| **Discovery and requirements** | The questions you ask before designing: users, volumes, data sensitivity, budget, what "done" means |
| **Trade-offs and economics** | Build or buy, managed or self-run, cost at scale, and the point where your choice would flip |
| **Security and governance** | Identity, data boundaries, audit, compliance, and for AI systems: prompt injection, PII and model risk |
| **Migration and legacy** | A path from today's system to the target without a big-bang cut-over, and how you roll back |
| **Case presentation** | Some loops ask for a take-home design you present to a panel, then defend under questions |
| **Influence and leadership** | Stories about getting teams to adopt a standard, disagreeing with a senior stakeholder, a design that failed |

## Which architect role?

| Role | The design questions lean towards |
|---|---|
| **Solutions architect** | Customer discovery, fitting a platform to a client's systems, demos and proofs of concept |
| **AI architect** | RAG and agent platforms, evaluation, guardrails, model choice, multi-tenant AI services |
| **Data architect** | Databricks or Snowflake lakehouse and warehouse design, modelling, Unity Catalog or Horizon governance, streaming versus batch, data products |
| **Cloud / enterprise architect** | Landing zones, networking, identity, reliability targets, cost governance across teams |

## How to prepare with OfferReady

1. **Add the job.** Paste the job link. You get what the role asks for, how
   your resume matches, and a plan up to your interview date.
   [Add a job →](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/analyze)
2. **Run the architect trade-off drills.** You make a design call, then the
   interviewer pushes back with "why?", a new constraint and an incident.
   The AI Architect (multi-tenant platform), Data Architect (Snowflake
   Cortex) and Databricks lakehouse drills fit this role directly, and you can
   write a drill for your exact job. [Open trade-off drills →](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/defend)
3. **Practise the questions for that job.** Questions are written from the job
   and your gaps, and each answer is graded against a rubric.
4. **Prepare your influence stories.** Write STAR stories for leadership,
   conflict and failure, then practise them out loud in a voice mock.
5. **Run a system design mock.** A voice round with a whiteboard to sketch on,
   with follow-ups on what you actually said.
