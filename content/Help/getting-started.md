# Getting started

Your first job, start to finish, in about five minutes.

## 1. Open the app and sign in

Go to the [OfferReady app](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/). You can
look around, watch the [one-minute demo](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/demo)
and [try a sample question](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/try)
without an account.

To save jobs and use the AI features, choose **Sign in** in the top right:

- **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**, or
- your **email and a password**. New accounts get an email to confirm the
  address first. Forgot your password? Type your email, then choose
  **Forgot password?** for a reset link.

After you sign in, OfferReady opens your job. If you don't have one yet, it
opens **Add a job**.

!!! tip "Want it like an app?"
    You can install OfferReady on your computer or phone, with no app store.
    See [Get the app](get-the-app.md).

## 2. Add a job

Choose **Add a job** in the top bar, then pick one:

- **Paste a link.** Links from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and
  SmartRecruiters import directly, with the exact job title.
- **Paste the description.** For LinkedIn and most other sites, open the
  posting, copy the job text and paste it. You can type the job title and
  company, or leave the title empty and OfferReady takes it from the text.

Add the **interview date** if you know it. Your plan is built backwards from
it. No date yet? Skip it and add it later from the job page.

Choose **Add this job**. In a few seconds you get what the role asks for, how
your resume matches, and a day-by-day plan.

## 3. Add your resume

On the same screen, under **Your resume**, upload a PDF, Word (.docx), text
or Markdown file, or paste the text. An old .doc file may not read: save it
as .docx or PDF first.

- You add it **once**. Every job, the tailoring tool and the mock interviews
  reuse it.
- It's read in your browser and saved on this device. It's stored on your
  account only if you turn on resume sync ([Your data and sync](your-data.md)).
- Replace it any time with **Replace**, or remove it with **Remove**.

## 4. Finish the one-time setup

Your job page has a **Set up once** box. It stays open until these are done,
then folds away:

1. **Job added**
2. **Interview date**
3. **Resume added**
4. **Gaps checked**: the resume match for this job
5. **Tailor your resume** (optional): rewrite your bullets for this job

## 5. Practise every day

The job page's **Every day** section shows the four practice steps in a
suggested order, with a **Next** tag on the one to do now:

1. **Questions for this job**: answer 5 out loud
2. **Trade-off drill**: hold one design call under push-back
3. **Your stories**: write one STAR story for the behavioral round
4. **Mock interview**: run one, start to finish

Skip any step you don't need. [Practice](practice.md) explains each one.

Also on the job page:

- **Do this next**: the single most useful thing to do right now.
- **Your plan for today**: about 40 minutes at most, always starting with
  **Answer 3 questions for this job**. Skills you're missing (dbt, Kafka)
  get **Study**; experience you're missing ("leading teams") gets **Prepare
  your answer**, which opens the AI Coach on that question so you can shape an
  honest answer from your closest real example. Tick
  them off as you go.
- **Readiness**: your score for this job, and your ready date once you've
  practised for a few days.

!!! tip "Short on time?"
    Do **Do this next** on the job page every day. It's the shortest path to
    ready.

## 6. On the day

Use **Interview notes** during the real interview: type each question as it's
asked, with no AI and no microphone. Afterwards, turn the notes into a
debrief. See [After the interview](after-the-interview.md).

## Common questions

??? question "Do I need an account?"
    No, not to look around. The demo, the sample question, the sample report,
    the study library and the question bank work without one. You need to sign
    in to save a job and use the AI features.

??? question "Can I add more than one job?"
    The free plan and the Job pass keep 1 saved job. The 30-day pass keeps 5,
    the 90-day pass 15 and the 1-year pass 40. See
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "I don't know my interview date yet"
    Add the job without it. You still get the match, the gaps and a plan. Add
    the date later on the job page. The plan then fits the days you have.

??? question "Can I use a LinkedIn job link?"
    LinkedIn links don't import. Open the posting, copy the description and
    use **Paste the description**.

??? question "Can I change the job title?"
    Yes. Choose **Rename** next to the title on the job page.
