# Getting started

Your first job, start to finish, in about five minutes.

## 1. Open the app

Go to the [OfferReady app](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/). You can
look around without an account. To save jobs and use the AI features, choose
**Sign in** (email, Google or GitHub).

## 2. Add a job

Choose **Add a job** in the top bar, then either:

- **paste the job link** (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and
  SmartRecruiters links import directly), or
- **paste the job description** (LinkedIn and most other sites: copy the text
  of the posting and paste it).

Add the **interview date** if you know it. The plan is built backwards from it.

## 3. Add your resume

Upload a PDF, Word or text file, or paste the text. You add it once and every
job uses it. It stays in your browser unless you turn on resume sync.

## 4. Read your job page

You'll see what the role needs, how your resume matches, the gaps to close
first, and **one next step**. Follow the next step each time you come back.

## 5. Practise, then rehearse

Use any of the practice tools (none is required), then run a mock interview.
Your readiness score and ready date update as you go.

!!! tip "Short on time?"
    Do the next step on the job page every day. That's the shortest path to
    ready.
