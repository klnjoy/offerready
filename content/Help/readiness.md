# Readiness

One score per job, from 0 to 100, and a forecast of the day you'll be ready.
You'll find it on each job page, and for all your jobs under **Readiness** in
the top bar.

## What goes into the score

| Part | Weight | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| **Resume match** | 50% | How well your resume covers what this job asks for |
| **Practice** | 30% | How your questions, drills, stories and mocks for this job scored |
| **Preparation** | 20% | How much of your plan you've completed |

The score uses only practice done **for that job**, so check the right job is
selected when you practise.

**Ready** means a score of **80**. Below 50 is weak, 50–74 is mid, 75 and
above is good.

## The ready date

OfferReady looks at how your score moved over the **last 14 days**, with
recent days counting more, and projects when you'll reach 80. Then it checks
that against your interview date:

- **On track:** ready before the interview, with the days to spare.
- **Running late:** how many days late, and roughly how many extra minutes a
  day would close the gap.
- **Stalled:** your score hasn't moved lately. Practise a little and the
  forecast comes back.

You need at least two readiness updates in the last two weeks before a
forecast appears ("Practise twice to unlock your forecast"). If you stop for
more than a week, the forecast assumes your pace slows down.

## The readiness page

**Readiness** in the top bar (**Progress** on a phone) shows, for each job,
in this order:

- **Your score** and what it means;
- **Readiness forecast**: your ready date against your interview date. With
  no date set yet, **Set your interview date** is here;
- **Your top 3 this week**: the three things that would raise your score
  most, biggest gains first. Tap a row to start it; **+10** is roughly how
  many points it can add;
- **What makes up your score**: resume, practice and stories, each with its
  part of the score;
- **Your score over time**;
- **Your skills for this job**: how each of the job's skills is going.

## Export a report

**Export** at the top of the readiness page saves the page as a PDF report
(your score, the ready date, what makes up the score and your top 3 this
week), dated and named for the job. On a phone, choose **Export**, then
**Save to Files** or share it. It always prints light, even in dark mode.

## How to raise your score

1. **Close resume gaps**: [tailor your resume](jobs.md#tailor-your-resume),
   then re-check the match. Resume match is half the score.
2. **Practise for this job**: questions, a drill, a story, a mock. Retrying a
   weak answer counts.
3. **Keep up with the plan**: tick today's tasks on the job page.
4. **Save mocks to readiness**: after a voice mock, choose **Save to
   readiness**.

## Common questions

??? question "My score didn't change after practising"
    Check the practice was for the same job (the job name is shown at the top
    of each practice page). Voice mocks only count after **Save to
    readiness**. Scores can take a moment to update after you finish.

??? question "Why is my score low even though I practised a lot?"
    Half the score is resume match. If your resume doesn't show what the job
    asks for, tailor it and re-check the match.

??? question "There's no ready date"
    You need some practice in the last 14 days. Practise twice and the
    forecast appears.

??? question "Does a high score mean I'll get the job?"
    No. Readiness is guidance on what to do next, not a prediction of the
    result. See the [Disclaimer](../Disclaimer/index.md).
