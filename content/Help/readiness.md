# Readiness

One score per job, from 0 to 100, and a forecast of the day you'll be ready.

## What goes into the score

- **Resume match** for this job;
- **Practice:** questions, drills, stories and mocks you've done for it, and
  how they scored;
- **Preparation:** progress through your plan.

## The ready date

OfferReady looks at your pace over the **last 14 days of practice** and
projects when you'll reach the ready mark. It then checks that against your
interview date:

- **On track:** ready before the interview, with the days to spare;
- **Running late:** how many days late, and roughly how many extra minutes a
  day would close the gap.

No practice in the last two weeks means no forecast yet. Practise a little
and it appears.

!!! note "Guidance, not a promise"
    Readiness helps you decide what to do next. It isn't a prediction of
    whether you'll get the job. See the [Disclaimer](../Disclaimer/index.md).
