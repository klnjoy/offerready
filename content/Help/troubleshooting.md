# Troubleshooting

Find your problem below. Each one says what you'll see and how to fix it. If
nothing here helps, see [Still stuck?](#still-stuck) at the end.

!!! tip "First, try this"
    Many problems go away with a **hard refresh**: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or
    Cmd+Shift+R (Mac). If you installed the app, close it and open it again.

## Signing in

??? question "I didn't get the confirmation email"
    Check spam and promotions. Make sure you typed the address correctly, then
    try **Create account** again. Or use **Continue with Google** or
    **Continue with GitHub** instead.

??? question "I forgot my password"
    On the sign-in form, type your email, then choose **Forgot password?**. A
    reset link is emailed to you.

??? question "\"Your session expired. Sign in again and retry\""
    Your sign-in timed out. Choose **Account** in the top right, sign in again,
    then repeat what you were doing. Work you did is saved on this device.

??? question "I signed in but my jobs are missing"
    Check you used the same sign-in method as before. Google, GitHub and email
    can be separate accounts if they use different email addresses. Then see
    [My progress isn't on my other device](#sync-and-your-data).

## Adding a job

??? question "My job link won't import"
    Links from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and SmartRecruiters import
    directly. LinkedIn and most other sites don't. Open the posting, copy the
    job description, switch to **Paste the description** and paste it.

    - **"That doesn't look like a link"**: copy the full address, starting
      with `https://`.
    - **"We couldn't read a job description from that page"**: the site
      blocks reading or needs a login. Paste the description instead.
    - **"Sign in to import from a link"**: importing from a link needs an
      account (it's free). Or paste the description.

??? question "\"Please paste the job description (at least a few sentences)\""
    The text is too short to analyze. Paste the whole posting: duties,
    requirements and nice-to-haves.

??? question "\"Analysis failed\" or \"The analysis came back empty\""
    Choose **Add this job** again: a second try usually works. If it keeps
    failing, check your connection, or paste the description instead of a
    link.

??? question "I can't add another job"
    The free plan and the Job pass keep 1 saved job. Delete one you no longer
    need from **Jobs**, or get a pass that keeps more. See
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "The job title or company is wrong"
    Choose **Rename** next to the title on the job page.

## Your resume

??? question "\"Couldn't read enough text from that file\""
    The file is probably a scanned PDF (a picture of text). Open your resume,
    select all the text, copy it and paste it into the resume box instead.

??? question "\"Unsupported file\""
    Upload a PDF, Word .docx, .txt or .md file. Old Word .doc files: save as
    .docx or PDF first, or paste the text.

??? question "\"Could not load the PDF reader\""
    The PDF reader loads when you pick a PDF and needs a connection. Check
    you're online and try again, or paste the text.

??? question "My resume isn't on my other device"
    Your resume stays on the device you added it on, unless you turn on resume
    sync: **Account** → **Your data** → **Also sync my saved resume to my
    account**. See [Your data and sync](your-data.md#resume-sync).

## Practice and AI features

??? question "Generating questions failed"
    Try **Write my questions** again: a second attempt usually works. **Get
    more questions** adds to your set and keeps the ones you have. If you see
    "Sign in … to get questions", sign in first.

??? question "\"Too many requests right now\""
    You sent a lot in a short time. Wait a minute and try again.

??? question "\"Couldn't reach the server\" or \"Check your connection\""
    You're offline or the connection dropped. Your work is saved on this
    device. When you're back online, try again.

??? question "\"… isn't available right now\" or \"couldn't build an answer this time\""
    A temporary problem with the AI service. Try again in a moment. In the
    question bank, **Show the model answer and rate yourself** works without
    AI.

??? question "A practice step doesn't get its tick"
    Ticks need the step's goal for **the selected job**: 5 answered questions,
    one finished drill, one story suited to the job, or one finished mock.
    Check the job shown at the top of the practice page.

??? question "My score seems wrong"
    Every score quotes your own words as evidence. Open the feedback to see
    which criteria were missing. Very short answers, and answers that skip the
    key point (for example the trade-off), are capped. In drills and the
    question bank you can rate yourself if you disagree.

??? question "A finished drill says it was saved to this browser only"
    You weren't signed in, the connection dropped, or you have several jobs and
    none was selected. Sign in, choose the job at the top of **Trade-off
    drills**, and run it again so it counts toward that job.

??? question "I left a drill in the middle"
    Open **Trade-off drills**. An **Unfinished drill** card lets you
    **Continue** or **Discard** it.

## Voice and microphone

??? question "The voice interview doesn't hear me"
    1. Use **Chrome** or **Edge** on a computer.
    2. When the browser asks, choose **Allow** for the microphone.
    3. Check the right microphone is selected in your computer's sound
       settings, and that it isn't muted.
    4. The setup screen shows a microphone level. Speak and look for "We can
       hear you".

    You can always tick **Type my answers instead of speaking**.

??? question "\"Microphone access was blocked\""
    You (or your browser) said no earlier. Click the microphone or lock icon
    in the address bar, set the microphone to **Allow**, then reload the page.

??? question "\"No microphone found\""
    Plug in a microphone or headset, or check your laptop's microphone is
    turned on in system settings. Or type your answers.

??? question "It stops listening too early"
    Set **Auto-stop after silence** to 5 or 8 seconds, or **Off** and press
    **Done** yourself.

??? question "I can't hear the interviewer"
    Check your volume and that **Mute the interviewer** is off. Under
    **Interviewer voice**, choose another voice and press **Test**.

??? question "\"Couldn't reach the interviewer. This answer is self-rated\""
    The AI service didn't answer for that question. Rate it yourself; the next
    question tries again.

## Sync and your data

??? question "My progress isn't on my other device"
    1. Sign in to the **same account** on both devices.
    2. Open **Account** → **Your data** on each and check the sync status.
       If it says **Not synced**, choose **Try again**. If it says **Sync is
       paused on this device**, choose **Turn sync back on**.
    3. Your resume only syncs with resume sync on. Interview notes stay on
       the device you typed them on until you turn them into a debrief.

??? question "Something I deleted came back"
    It synced back from another device that still had it. Delete it there
    too, or make sure that device has synced first.

??? question "I cleared my browser data"
    Sign in again and your synced data comes back. Things kept only on that
    device (interview notes, recent voice sessions, an unsynced resume) can't
    be recovered.

## App, install and display

??? question "The page looks old or a new feature is missing"
    Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac). In the
    installed app, choose **Update available** when it appears, or close and
    reopen the app.

??? question "I can't install the app"
    Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, Chrome on Android, or Safari on iPhone.
    See [Get the app](get-the-app.md).

??? question "The app icon or tab icon is the old one"
    Browsers and phones cache icons. Hard refresh the page. On a phone,
    remove the app from your home screen and add it again.

??? question "Something doesn't fit on my screen"
    Zoom the browser back to 100% (Ctrl+0 or Cmd+0). If it still looks wrong,
    send us a screenshot with **Send feedback**.

## Limits and payments

??? question "It says I've used my limit"
    Free limits reset at the start of each month. A one-time pass raises them.
    Mock interview packs add voice mocks that never expire. See
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "I paid but still see the free plan"
    Reload the page. If it still says Free after a minute, sign out and back
    in with the account you paid with. If it's still wrong, [contact
    us](../Contact/index.md) with the email on your receipt.

??? question "\"Couldn't start checkout\" or \"Couldn't open billing\""
    Try again in a moment. Check that pop-up or tracker blockers aren't
    blocking Stripe's checkout page.

??? question "Where are my receipts?"
    **Account** → **Manage billing** opens Stripe's customer portal with your
    invoices and receipts.

## Still stuck?

1. **Ask OfferReady**: the help assistant in the bottom-right corner of the
   app. It knows which screen you're on.
2. **Send feedback**: in the account menu and the footer. Include what you
   did, what you expected, and a screenshot if you can.
3. [Contact us](../Contact/index.md).
