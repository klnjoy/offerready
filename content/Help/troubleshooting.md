# Troubleshooting and FAQ

??? question "The page looks old or a new feature is missing"
    The app can be installed and keeps an offline copy. Do a hard refresh
    (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac). If you installed it, close and
    reopen the app.

??? question "My job link won't import"
    Links from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and SmartRecruiters import
    directly. For LinkedIn and other sites, open the posting, copy the job
    description and paste it. Use **Clear** to remove a link you pasted.

??? question "The job title is wrong"
    Rename the job from its page. Imported jobs use the exact title from the
    posting.

??? question "Generating questions failed"
    Try again; a second attempt usually works. **Get more questions** adds to
    your set and keeps the ones you have.

??? question "The voice interview doesn't hear me"
    Use Chrome or Edge on desktop, allow microphone access when asked, and
    check the right microphone is selected in your system settings. You can
    also tick **Type my answers instead of speaking**.

??? question "My progress isn't on my other device"
    Make sure you're signed in to the same account on both, and that sync
    isn't paused on either device (the sync status in the top bar). Your resume only syncs if
    you've turned on resume sync.

??? question "It says I've used my limit"
    Free limits reset each month. A one-time pass raises them; see
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "Something else"
    Ask **Ask OfferReady** in the bottom-right corner of the app, use
    **Send feedback**, or [contact us](../Contact/index.md).
