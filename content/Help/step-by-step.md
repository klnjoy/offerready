# Step-by-step guide

This guide walks through OfferReady one screen at a time, in plain words. You
don't need any technical knowledge. Follow it in order the first time; after
that, jump to the part you need.

!!! tip "Two things to know first"
    - **OfferReady is for practice only.** Use it before and after your
      interviews, never during a live one.
    - **On a phone, the bar at the bottom is how you get around:** Home,
      Practice, AI Coach, Progress and More, plus a green **+** button for
      quick actions.

## Part 1. Get started (about 5 minutes, once)

### Step 1. Open the app

1. Go to [klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/).
2. Optional: watch the [one-minute demo](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/demo)
   to see every feature.
3. Want it like a phone app? Follow [Get the app](get-the-app.md). On iPhone:
   open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

### Step 2. Sign in

1. Tap **Sign in** (top right).
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub**, or type your email
   and a password.
3. New with email? Open the confirmation email and tap the link, then come back.

### Step 3. Add the job you're interviewing for

1. Tap the green **+** button, then **Add a job**.
2. Paste the **link** to the job posting. If the link doesn't work (LinkedIn,
   for example), open the posting, copy all the text, and paste that instead.
3. Add the **interview date** if you know it. No date yet? Skip it.
4. Tap **Add this job**. Wait a few seconds.

You now see what the job asks for, the skills you're missing (your "gaps") and
a plan for each day until the interview.

### Step 4. Add your resume

1. On the same screen, under **Your resume**, tap **Upload PDF, DOCX or TXT** and pick your
   resume file (PDF or Word), or paste the text.
2. You get a **resume match** score: how well your resume fits this job, and
   which skills the job wants that your resume doesn't show.
3. Optional: tap **Tailor your resume** to rewrite your bullet points with the
   job's words. It never invents experience. Afterwards it asks whether to save
   the changes to your main resume too, and you can download it as a Word file.

## Part 2. Practise a little every day (15 to 30 minutes)

Open **Home**. The top of the screen shows today's tasks. Tap the first one and
follow it; each task is a few minutes.

On **Practice** you work in **rounds**. One round is 3 questions, a trade-off
drill, a story and a mock interview, in any order and on any days. When a
round is done, tap **Start round 2**: the steps reset and you get new
questions. Repeat rounds until your interview.

### Step 5. Answer the questions for this job

1. Tap the **Practice** tab, then **Questions for this job**.
2. Pick a question. Before you answer, open **Hint: how to answer** to see how
   to shape a good answer for that kind of question.
3. Say your answer out loud, then type the main points.
4. Tap **Get feedback**. You see a score, what was good, and what was missing.
5. Tap **Try again** to improve it, or **Show strong answer** to compare.
6. Feedback can link to **Study notes for this**: tap it to read the topic in
   our free study library.

### Step 6. Practise trade-offs

Interviewers often ask "why did you choose X and not Y?".

1. **Practice** tab → **Trade-off drill**.
2. Make your choice, then defend it while the interviewer pushes back.
3. **Show strong answer** shows a model answer. Tap **See it with an
   architecture flow and code** to get a diagram you can draw and a short code
   example, built from your own resume.

### Step 7. Write your stories

Behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time…") need real stories.

1. Tap **+** → **Quick story**.
2. Fill in three boxes: **What happened?**, **What did you do?** and **How did
   it end?** Writing it yourself is free.
3. Or open **Or let AI draft it from a few words** (uses 1 story credit) and
   check every part it fills in.
4. Tap **Save story**. Aim for 6 to 10 stories in total.

### Step 8. Get help from the AI Coach

1. Tap the **AI Coach** tab.
2. Type any interview question.
3. The Coach builds an answer from **your** resume and stories, and labels where
   each point comes from. It never invents experience. For design questions it
   adds an architecture flow; for coding questions, a short code example.
4. Tap **Practise saying it** to rehearse it in your own words.

### Step 9. Do a mock interview

1. **Practice** tab → **Mock interview**.
2. Pick the type (behavioral, technical, system design or mixed), the length
   and the interviewer's style.
3. **Voice** (you speak) uses 3 mock credits. **Text** (you type, under
   **Voice and feedback settings → Type my answers**) uses 1. The screen shows
   how many credits you have left.
4. Tap **Start session** and answer. The interviewer asks follow-ups on what you
   actually said.
5. At the end you get scores for content and delivery, what to fix, and links
   to the study notes for each topic.

### Step 10. Check your progress

1. Tap the **Progress** tab.
2. You see one readiness score per job and the day you're likely to be ready,
   compared with your interview date.
3. Tap **Export PDF** to keep a copy.
4. To see what you did yesterday (or any day), open **Practice → Your practice
   log**. It lists each day's questions, drills, stories and mocks, with a
   link to do more.

## Part 3. Interview day and after

### Step 11. Take notes during the interview

1. Just before the interview, tap **+** → **Interview notes**.
2. The round is already set from your last debrief (you can change it). If you
   had an earlier round, tap **Carry into these notes** to bring over what went
   badly last time.
3. When they ask a question, type it and press **Enter**, or open **Likely in a
   … round for this job** and tap the question if it's there.
4. Under **Questions you asked them**, add what you asked, or tap one of the
   ideas. Your questions are saved for next time.
5. Afterwards, tap **Done: turn into a debrief**.

### Step 12. Debrief while it's fresh (2 minutes)

1. For each question, tap **Good**, **OK** or **Bad**, then tap a quick note
   like **Blanked** or **Good example** instead of typing.
2. Tap how it felt overall, and the outcome (**Waiting to hear**, **Next
   round**, **Offer** or **Rejected**). For **Next round**, add the date.
3. Tap **Save debrief**. Questions that went badly come back in your plan to
   practise. Your plan moves to the next round's date.
4. Want to send it to a mentor or friend? On the saved round, tap **Share** and
   choose email, WhatsApp, text, LinkedIn or X. Interviewer names and your
   private notes are not included.

### Step 13. Keep the questions you were asked

1. **More** → **Questions you were asked** shows every question from every
   interview.
2. Add one directly with **Add a question you were asked**.
3. Tap **Practise with AI Coach** on any question that went badly.

### Step 13b. Look back at past jobs

1. **Jobs** → **Job history** (or **More** → **Job history**).
2. Each job shows its outcome and what you did for it. Tap a job to see every
   question, drill, mock, story, debrief and saved answer, with links.
3. Search by job or company, or filter by **Offer**, **Rejected** or
   **Deleted**. Deleted jobs keep their history here.

### Step 14. Compare offers

1. **More** → **Compare offers** → **Add an offer**.
2. Tap a job under **Start from a job you saved** to fill in the company, title
   and level, then add the salary, bonus and stock (quick buttons help).
3. Add a second offer to see them side by side, then ask for a negotiation plan.

## Part 4. Find roles and keep learning

### Open roles and alerts

1. **Home** → **Open roles** (or **More** → **Open roles**).
2. Filter by role, skill, **state**, **posted in the last 3 or 7 days**,
   **Sponsors visas**, **Contract / C2C** or **Shows pay**.
3. Tap **Prepare** on a role to add it as your job, or **Apply** to open the
   company's own page.
4. Tap **Alert me about new roles like these** to get a daily notification when
   new matching roles are posted. Notifications need to be turned on. On
   iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first.

### The 12-week AI roadmap

1. **More** → **12-week AI roadmap**.
2. Each week has what to learn, a study-library chapter, a small project and
   the questions to practise. Weeks 1 to 8 include Python you can run on the
   page.
3. At the bottom: **Free courses worth your time**.

### Question of the day

Home shows one interview question a day. Tap **Send me one every morning** to
get it as a notification.

## Part 5. Plans and help

- **Free**: 1 saved job and a monthly allowance, including 1 mock credit (one
  text mock). See [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).
- **Passes** are paid once and never renew: 30-day ($19), 90-day ($59) and
  1-year ($99). A **mock pack** adds 10 mock credits.
- Stuck? **More** → **Help** to ask how anything works, or **Send feedback** to
  tell us. See also [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
