# AI Interview Coach

Ask any interview question and get an answer built from your resume and
stories, for the job you're preparing for. It's a practice tool: you use it
before the interview to work out what you'd say, then practise saying it
yourself. It never runs during a live interview.

!!! tip "See it first"
    [Try a sample question](https://getofferready.app/try) with no
    account, or watch the seven-step walkthrough on the
    [demo page](https://getofferready.app/demo#coach).

## Before you start

The coach is only as good as what it can read. Add these first:

- **A job** (it reads what the role asks for and your gaps);
- **Your resume**;
- **A few STAR stories** ([Your stories](practice.md#3-your-stories)).

The **Using** line at the top shows the job, whether your resume is saved
(**✓ Resume** or **+ Add resume**), how many stories you have.
Tap any of them to change or add it.

## How to use it

1. Open **Coach**. Check the job in the **Using** line.
2. Work through **Get these answers ready**: the answers to have ready for
   this job ("Tell me about yourself", one question for each gap between your
   resume and the job, and "Why this role"). The bar shows how many are ready.
   Each question says **Build**, **Ready** (saved) or your last practice score.
   Tap **Build** to get an answer; tap a saved one to see it and practise it
   again (no new AI call). To ask something else, use **Or ask your own
   question**. Your job's full question list is in **Practice › Questions for
   this job**; open a question there and choose **See an answer built from
   your resume** to bring it here.
3. Read the answer:
   - **30 seconds** and **Full answer**: two lengths of the same answer to
     say out loud;
   - **Remember**: the three things to keep in mind when you say it;
   - **Where this comes from**: your best example and every point with its
     source (below).
4. Change it with one tap: **Shorter**, **Add numbers**, **STAR format**,
   **More technical** or **Different example**.
5. **Practise saying it**: answer in your own words and get scored. The
   coach's answer stays hidden unless you open it.
6. Tap a question under **They'll likely ask next** to continue in the same
   thread, or **Start a new question**.

## Reading the answer

Every point in the outline has a **source label**:

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| **Your resume** / **Your story** | From your material, with the words it came from. The quote is checked against your real text. If it isn't there, the label changes. |
| **Your words** | From what you typed. |
| **General knowledge** | General technical knowledge, not a claim about you. |
| **Suggestion: only if true for you** | Something you could say, but only if it really happened. |

**Numbers are checked.** Any figure in the answer that isn't in your
resume, stories or draft is flagged under **Check these numbers**. Use your
real figure or cut it. Anything in **[brackets]** is a detail for you to fill
in. The coach never invents your numbers silently.

## Then make it yours

- **Make it more concise:** a shorter version.
- **Challenge it:** the push-back an interviewer would give.
- **Practise this question:** answer it in your own words and get rubric
  feedback. Try again until it's yours.
- **Save to my answers:** keep the best version for this job under
  **Your answers**, next to the question.
- **Copy:** copy the answer text.

## Common questions

??? question "Can I use the coach during a real interview?"
    No. It's designed for practice only, and OfferReady has no live mode.
    Interviewers ask "why?" until rehearsed or AI-fed answers break. The coach
    helps you build answers you can defend yourself. In the real interview,
    use the plain [Interview notes](after-the-interview.md#take-notes-during-the-interview).

??? question "The answer is generic"
    The coach needs your material. Add your resume and two or three STAR
    stories, and check the right job is selected. Without them, it can only
    offer general knowledge.

??? question "It used a number I never gave it"
    It shouldn't present one as yours. Such numbers are listed under **Check
    these numbers**. Replace them with your real figure or remove them. If a
    number was used without a flag, please tell us with **Send feedback**.

??? question "Does it use my allowance?"
    Yes. Each answer uses one AI feedback from your plan. See
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "Where are my saved answers?"
    In the AI Interview Coach under **Your answers**, for the selected job.
    They sync to your account while you're signed in.
