# AI Interview Coach

Ask any interview question and get an answer built from your resume and
stories, for the job you're preparing for. It's for practice, never for use
during a live interview.

## How to use it

1. Open **AI Coach** in the top bar and pick the job.
2. Type a question, or choose one of the suggested questions from that job.
3. Choose what you want:

| Mode | You get |
|---|---|
| **Answer** | A speakable answer outline |
| **Example** | The best real example from your resume or stories, and how to use it |
| **STAR** | A behavioral answer in situation, task, action, result form |
| **Trade-off** | The key trade-offs, tied to your experience |
| **System design** | Requirements through architecture, reliability, security and cost |
| **Follow-ups** | The questions an interviewer would ask next, and how to handle them |
| **Improve** | Your own draft, tightened and completed |

## Reading the answer

Every point has a **source label**:

- **Resume** or **Story**: quoted from your material. The quote is checked
  against your real text; if it isn't there, the point is relabelled.
- **You**: from the draft you wrote.
- **General**: general technical knowledge, not a claim about you.
- **Suggestion**: something you could say *if it's true for you*.

Numbers in "Say it like this" that don't appear anywhere in your material are
**flagged**. Replace them with your real figures before you use them. Gaps in
square brackets, like `[X%]`, are for you to fill in.

## Then practise it

- **Practise this question:** answer it yourself and get rubric feedback.
- **Save to my answers:** keep your best version for this job under
  **Your answers**.
- **Make it more concise** or **Challenge it** for a shorter version or
  follow-up pressure.

!!! note "Better input, better answers"
    Add your resume and a few STAR stories first. Without them, the coach can
    only offer general advice and suggestions.

Each answer uses one of your monthly AI feedback answers. See
