# Mock interviews

Rehearse the real thing. Choose **Mock interview** in the top bar. It's step 4
of the [practice path](practice.md), so the path is shown at the top. Pick
**voice** or **text**.

!!! tip "See a report first"
    The [sample report](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/sample-report)
    shows what you get after a voice mock, on sample data.

## Voice interview

You answer out loud. The interviewer listens, then follows up on what you
actually said.

### Set it up

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Job** | One of your jobs, or **General practice (no job)** |
| **Interview type** | Behavioral, Technical deep dive, System design, or Mixed (all three, like a real loop) |
| **Length** | 3 or 5 questions |
| **Interviewer style** | Friendly, Neutral, or Tough (a bar-raiser who presses on vague claims) |
| **Follow-up depth** | Normal (one follow-up when needed) or **Deep: keep asking why** (up to 3 chained follow-ups) |

Under **Voice and feedback settings**:

- **Interviewer voice**: pick a voice and choose **Test** to hear it.
- **Mute the interviewer**: read the questions instead of hearing them.
- **Auto-stop after silence**: 3, 5 or 8 seconds, or **Off** (press **Done**).
- **Show feedback after each answer**: turn it off for feedback only at the
  end, like a real interview.
- **Type my answers instead of speaking**: for when you can't talk out loud.

Choose **Start session**. Your browser asks to use the microphone the first
time.

### During the session

1. The interviewer asks the question. Choose the microphone button and answer.
2. When you stop talking it stops listening on its own, or choose **Done**.
3. A follow-up may come next, about what you said. The header shows
   "Question 2 of 5 · follow-up".
4. **System design** rounds have a **whiteboard** to sketch boxes and arrows.
   Your sketch is sent with your answer.

### The report

At the end you get:

- an **overall score** and four scores: **structure**, **depth**,
  **relevance** and **communication**;
- **delivery**: pace in words a minute, filler words, long pauses, how many
  answers were a good length, and "I" versus "we" in behavioral answers;
- your **best** and **weakest** answer;
- each question with your transcript, the follow-ups and feedback.

Then:

- **Save to readiness**: adds this mock's score to the job's readiness.
- **Practice weakest again**: re-run the question that went worst.
- **Download report** (printable), **.md**, or **Copy share summary**. The
  report is built on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- A **Next step** card shows what to do next on the practice path.
- **New session** starts another one.

## Text interview

A mixed loop of questions you answer in writing. Answer, compare with a strong
answer, then face the follow-up. Good when you can't talk out loud. At the end
you see what to focus on next, and **Run another mock**.

## Ending early

In a voice interview, **End interview** is always there. In a text interview
it's **End mock**. Either way you get feedback on the questions you answered.

## Common questions

??? question "Which browser should I use?"
    Chrome or Edge on a computer works best for voice. On a phone, tick
    **Type my answers instead of speaking**.

??? question "Is my audio recorded or sent anywhere?"
    No. Speech recognition runs in your browser. OfferReady receives only the
    text of your answers, to give feedback, never your audio.

??? question "It cut me off while I was thinking"
    Raise **Auto-stop after silence** to 5 or 8 seconds, or set it to **Off**
    and press **Done** yourself.

??? question "It doesn't hear me"
    Allow the microphone when the browser asks, and check the right microphone
    is selected in your computer's sound settings. See
    [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#voice-and-microphone).

??? question "How many voice mocks can I do?"
    1 a month on the free plan, and 4 to 40 with a pass. A **Mock interview
    pack** adds 10 more that never expire. See
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "Where are my past sessions?"
    Recent voice sessions are kept in this browser, under **Recent sessions**
    on the voice interview screen. Use **Download report** to keep a copy.
