# Mock interviews

Rehearse the real thing. Choose **Mock interview** in the top bar, then voice
or text.

## Voice interview

Answer out loud. The interviewer listens, then asks about what you actually
said.

- **Round type:** behavioral, technical, system design, or a mixed loop.
- **Deep mode:** keeps asking "why?", up to three times.
- **System design** rounds come with a whiteboard to sketch on.
- **Feedback:** content and delivery (pace, length, filler words, and "I"
  versus "we" in behavioral answers).
- Prefer typing? Tick **Type my answers instead of speaking**.

!!! tip "Browser and microphone"
    Voice works best in Chrome or Edge on desktop. Your browser asks for
    microphone access when you start. Speech recognition runs in your browser;
    OfferReady never receives your audio.

Recent voice sessions are saved in your browser so you can review them.
**Save to readiness** sends only the score to your account.

## Text interview

A mixed loop of questions. Answer, compare with a strong answer, then face the
follow-up. Good for practising when you can't talk out loud.

## Ending early

**End mock** is always available. You get feedback on the questions you
answered.
