# Get the app

OfferReady installs straight from the website, with no app store. It gets its
own icon and window, opens on your job, and keeps your work on the device.
It's built from the same code as the website, so both are always up to date.

You can also open [Get the app](https://klnjoy.github.io/offerready-app/app)
in OfferReady, or choose **Get the app** in the top bar. On a computer or
Android phone, that button installs it in one click.

## Install it

=== "Windows or Mac"

    1. Open OfferReady in **Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
    2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or open the
       browser menu and choose **Install OfferReady**.
    3. OfferReady opens in its own window. Find it in your Start menu, Dock or
       Launchpad like any app.

=== "Android"

    1. Open OfferReady in **Chrome**.
    2. Tap the menu (three dots) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home
       screen**.
    3. Open it from your home screen. It runs full screen, without the browser
       bar.

=== "iPhone or iPad"

    1. Open OfferReady in **Safari**.
    2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
    3. Open it from your home screen. It runs full screen, without the browser
       bar.

## Shortcuts

Right-click the app icon (on a computer) or long-press it (on a phone) for
shortcuts straight to **My jobs**, the **AI Interview Coach**, a **Mock
interview** and **Interview notes**, where your device supports it.

## What works offline

- **Works offline:** interview notes, your plan, and anything you've already
  opened.
- **Needs a connection:** AI features (the coach, answer feedback, writing
  questions, the voice interview) and syncing. Changes you make offline sync
  when you're back online.

## Updates

The app updates itself. You never need to refresh or reinstall to get a new
version:

- If an update arrives when you open the app or come back to it, it switches
  to the new version straight away.
- If it arrives while you're busy, you'll see **Update available.** Choose
  **Refresh** to update now, or carry on: it updates by itself the next time
  you leave the app.
- It never updates in the middle of a mock interview, or on a screen where
  you've typed something, so nothing you're writing is lost.

## Common questions

??? question "Is it a real app?"
    Yes. It installs, has its own icon and window, and keeps your work on the
    device. It's a web app (a PWA), so it doesn't come from an app store and
    there's nothing to pay for installing it.

??? question "I don't see the install option"
    On a computer, use Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari on Mac don't offer
    it). On iPhone you must use Safari. If OfferReady is already installed,
    the option is hidden. Open it from your apps instead.

??? question "The icon on my phone is the old one"
    Phones keep the icon from when you installed. Remove the app from your
    home screen and add it again to get the new icon. Your data stays, as long
    as you're signed in.

??? question "Do voice interviews work in the app on my phone?"
    Voice works best on a computer in Chrome or Edge. On a phone, tick **Type
    my answers instead of speaking**.

??? question "How do I uninstall it?"
    Like any app: drag it out of your Dock or Start menu, or long-press the
    icon on your phone and remove it. On a computer you can also open the
    app's menu (the three dots in its title bar) and choose **Uninstall**.
