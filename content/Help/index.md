# OfferReady Help Center

The complete manual for OfferReady: how every part of the app works, step by
step, plus answers to common questions and fixes for common problems.

OfferReady is a web app that helps you prepare for one real job interview at a
time. You add the job and your resume. OfferReady works out what the interview
will test, has you practise it, and shows the day you'll be ready.

!!! tip "Never used an app like this?"
    Start with the [Step-by-step guide](step-by-step.md): every screen in plain
    words, from opening the app to comparing offers.

## The loop: add a job, practise, rehearse

| Step | What you do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Set up once** | Add the job and your resume, set the interview date, check your gaps | [Getting started](getting-started.md) |
| **2. Practise every day** | 1 Questions for this job, 2 Trade-off drill, 3 Your stories, 4 Mock interview | [Practice](practice.md), [Mock interviews](mock-interviews.md) |
| **3. Interview day** | A plain notepad during the interview, then a debrief | [After the interview](after-the-interview.md) |

The [readiness score](readiness.md) and ready date update as you go.

## Words you'll see a lot

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Job** | One role you're preparing for. Everything else hangs off it. |
| **Gaps** | Things the job asks for that your resume doesn't clearly show yet. |
| **Plan** | A day-by-day list of tasks up to your interview date. |
| **Practice path** | The four suggested practice steps, in order. Every step can be skipped. |
| **Readiness** | One score per job (0–100) from your resume match, practice and plan progress. |
| **Ready date** | When you'll reach the ready mark at your current pace. |
| **Rubric** | The criteria an answer is graded on. Every score quotes your own words as evidence. |
| **Source label** | In the AI Interview Coach, where each point came from: your resume, a story, your own words, general knowledge, or a suggestion. |
| **Pass** | A one-time payment that raises your limits for a fixed number of days. |

## Find your way around the app

- **Top bar:** Jobs, Practice, AI Coach, Mock interview, Readiness, and **Add a job**.
- **Your job page:** opens after sign-in. Everything for one job is there.
- **Ask OfferReady:** the help assistant in the bottom-right corner of the
  app. It knows which screen and which job you're on. Its **FAQ** tab has
  quick answers.
- **Send feedback:** in the account menu and the footer. We read every message.

## Common questions

??? question "Is OfferReady free?"
    Yes. The free plan covers the full prep loop for one job, with no card:
    1 saved job, 3 job analyses a month, 5 AI answer feedbacks a month and
    1 voice mock a month. One-time passes raise the limits. See
    [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "Does OfferReady help during a live interview?"
    No, on purpose. OfferReady is for practice before the interview and a
    debrief after it. There is no live mode, overlay or answer feed, and
    nothing listens to your real interview. During the interview you can use
    [Interview notes](after-the-interview.md#take-notes-during-the-interview),
    a plain notepad with no AI and no microphone.

??? question "Do I have to do every practice step?"
    No. The four steps are a suggested order. Each page has a **Skip to …**
    link, and the job page says "Skip any step you don't need".

??? question "Where is my resume kept?"
    On your device by default. It's stored on your account only if you turn on
    resume sync. See [Your data and sync](your-data.md).

??? question "Which roles is OfferReady for?"
    Technical roles: AI/ML, data and platform engineers, forward deployed
    engineers, staff and principal engineers, and solutions, AI, data and
    cloud architects. See the role guides under **For your role**.

??? question "Something isn't working"
    See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md), ask **Ask OfferReady** in the
    app, or [contact us](../Contact/index.md).
