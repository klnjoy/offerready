# OfferReady Help Center

What OfferReady does, the loop it runs on, and how to find your way around.

OfferReady is a web app that helps you prepare for one real job interview at a
time. You add the job and your resume; OfferReady works out what the interview
will test, practises you on it, and shows the day you'll be ready.

!!! tip "New here? Start with these"
    1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): your first job in about five minutes.
    2. [Jobs and your resume](jobs.md): what the job page shows you.
    3. [Practice](practice.md): which of the four tools to use, and when.

## The loop: add a job, practise, rehearse

| Step | What you do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Add a job** | Paste the job link or description, add your resume, set the interview date | [Jobs and your resume](jobs.md) |
| **2. Practise** | Questions for this job, trade-off drills, STAR stories, the question bank, and the AI Interview Coach | [Practice](practice.md), [AI Interview Coach](coach.md) |
| **3. Rehearse and check** | Voice or text mock interviews, then your readiness score and ready date | [Mock interviews](mock-interviews.md), [Readiness](readiness.md) |

After a real round, a [debrief](after-the-interview.md) sends anything that
went badly back into your plan for the next round.

## Words you'll see a lot

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Job** | One role you're preparing for. Everything else hangs off it. |
| **Gaps** | Things the job asks for that your resume doesn't clearly show yet. |
| **Plan** | A day-by-day list of tasks up to your interview date. |
| **Readiness** | One score per job (0–100) from your resume match, practice and preparation. |
| **Ready date** | When you'll reach the ready mark at your current pace. |
| **Rubric** | The criteria an answer is graded on. Every score quotes your own words as evidence. |
| **Source label** | In the AI Interview Coach, where each point came from: your resume, a story, your draft, general knowledge, or a suggestion. |

## Find your way around

- **Top bar:** Jobs, Practice, AI Coach, Mock interview, Readiness, plus **Add a job**.
- **Ask OfferReady:** the help assistant in the bottom-right corner of the app. It knows which screen and which job you're on.
- **Send feedback:** in the account menu and the footer. We read every message.

## Common questions

??? question "Is OfferReady free?"
    Yes, there's a free plan: one saved job, 3 job analyses a month, 5 AI
    feedback answers a month and one voice mock a month. One-time passes raise
    the limits. See [Plans and billing](account-billing.md).

??? question "Does it help during a live interview?"
    No. OfferReady is for practice before the interview and a debrief after
    it. There's no live mode, overlay or answer feed.

??? question "Where is my resume kept?"
    In your browser by default. It's only stored on your account if you turn
    on resume sync. See [Security and your data](../Security/index.md).

??? question "Something isn't working"
    Try [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md), or [contact us](../Contact/index.md).
