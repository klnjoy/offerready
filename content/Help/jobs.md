# Jobs and your resume

Every job you add gets its own page with three tabs.

## The job page

| Tab | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | What the role needs, your plan to the interview date, practice progress and the next step |
| **Resume** | How your resume matches the job, the gaps, and **Tailor your resume** |
| **After the interview** | Debriefs for each round, the outcome, and offer comparison |

## Adding a job from a link

Links from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and SmartRecruiters import
directly, with the exact job title. For LinkedIn and other sites, open the
posting, copy the description and paste it instead. If a link won't import,
use **Clear** and paste the text.

You can rename a job at any time from its page.

## Resume match and gaps

The match shows which requirements your resume clearly covers. A **gap** is a
requirement your resume doesn't show yet. Gaps aren't failures: they're the
topics most likely to come up as hard questions, so the plan sends you to them
first.

## Tailor your resume

On the **Resume** tab, **Tailor your resume** rewrites your bullets for this
job's skills and gaps without inventing anything. Review the suggestions,
accept the ones you want, copy them into your resume, then **re-check** the
match.

## Many jobs

**Jobs** in the top bar lists every job. The **Readiness** page shows one
score per job, so you can see which interview needs attention.
