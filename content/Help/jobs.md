# Jobs and your resume

Every job you add gets its own page. Everything for that job lives there: your
plan, practice progress, resume match and debriefs.

## The job page

Open a job from **Jobs** in the top bar. At the top you'll see the job title,
**Rename**, and **Switch job** to jump to another job. The page has four tabs.

### Overview

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Interview** | Your interview date and a countdown. **Save date** or **Change date**. |
| **Do this next** | The single most useful thing to do now, with one button. |
| **Every day** | The four practice steps in order, with ticks for the ones you've done and a **Next** tag. Any time: AI Interview Coach and Question bank. |
| **Your plan for today** | Today's tasks for this job. Tick them off as you finish. |
| **Readiness** | Your score for this job, your streak and tasks done this week. **Details** opens the full readiness page. |
| **Interview day** | **Interview notes** for the real interview, and **Log a round** afterwards. |
| **The plan to day N** | The days ahead, each with its tasks. **Add to calendar** and **Show all** days. |
| **Role guide** | A guide to how this type of interview is run. |
| **Set up once** | Job, date, resume, gaps checked, and the optional tailoring. It folds away once done. |
| **About this job** | What the role asks for, from the description. |

### Settings

A plain form for this job. Nothing changes until you press a button.

- **Job details**: job title, company and interview date. Change what you
  need, then choose **Save changes**, or **Cancel** to put them back. If you
  try to leave with changes you haven't saved, OfferReady warns you.
- **Delete this job**: asks you to confirm. Your resume and stories stay.

### Resume match

How your resume matches this job, your top gaps, your saved resume, and
**Tailor my resume for this job**. This is the one place to change your
resume: upload a file or paste the text, check it, then choose **Save
resume**. **Remove** asks first. (On a phone the tab is called **Resume**.)

### After the interview

Your debriefs for each round, the outcome, interview notes and **Compare
offers**. See [After the interview](after-the-interview.md).

!!! tip "Open a tab directly"
    Links keep the tab, so you can bookmark a job's Resume tab, for example.

## Getting around

Every job page and practice tool shows where you are, like the study notes:

- **On a phone**, **Today** shows what to do today for your job. Tap **Today ▾**
  for a calendar of your plan: dots mark plan days, a bright dot a finished
  day and an orange ring your interview. Pick a day to see its tasks, or set
  it as your interview day.
- **On a phone**, the bar at the bottom has **Today** (your job's plan),
  **Practice**, **Coach**, **Progress** (readiness) and **More** (your streak,
  and everything else: jobs, stories, debriefs, the study library, account,
  help and sign out). The round **+** button
  next to it has quick actions: **Add a job**, **Interview notes**, **Log a
  round**, **Quick story** and **Ask for help**.
- **The trail** at the top, for example
  *Jobs › Senior AI Engineer › Practise every day › Trade-off drill*. Choose
  any part to go up a level.
- **The folder list** on the left of a computer, like the study notes. Under
  **Your jobs**, each job is a folder, in the order you prepare:
  **Overview**, **Resume match**, **Practise every day** (the four steps,
  ticked when done), **AI Interview Coach**, **Any time** (Question bank,
  Readiness), **Interview day and after** (Interview notes during the
  interview, Debriefs to log a round and see past ones, Questions you were
  asked, Compare offers), and **Job settings** last. The
  folders you're in are open; choose any folder to open or close it.
- **On this page** on the right of a wide screen lists the sections of the
  page you're on.
- **Menu** on a phone or tablet: opens the same folders from the left.
  Choose a folder to go into it, and the back arrow to go up.
- **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom of each main step.

## Adding a job

Choose **Add a job** in the top bar.

1. **Paste a link** or **Paste the description**.
   - Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and SmartRecruiters links import
     directly, with the exact job title.
   - For LinkedIn and other sites, copy the job text and paste it.
   - To change a link you pasted, choose **Remove link** or **Use another link**.
2. Optionally type the **Job title** (as the posting says it) and **Company**.
   Leave the title empty and OfferReady takes it from the description.
3. Add your resume if you haven't yet. You only add it once.
4. Add the **Interview date** if you know it.
5. Choose **Add this job**.

You land on the job page with "Job added" and your match. Want to see the
format first? **See a sample first** shows a finished analysis on sample data.

## Managing your jobs

**Jobs** in the top bar lists every job you're preparing for.

- **Search and filter:** by title or company, by status (for example active
  jobs) and sort by most recent, oldest or next interview.
- **Current job:** the one OfferReady opens by default. Opening a job makes it
  current. Practice tools use the current job unless you pick another.
- **Delete:** removes the job **with its analysis and practice**. You're asked
  to confirm first.
- **Readiness** in the top bar shows one score per job, so you can see which
  interview needs attention.

## Resume match and gaps

The match compares the job's requirements with your resume:

- **Found in resume:** requirements your resume clearly shows, with the
  evidence.
- **Gaps:** requirements your resume doesn't show yet. Gaps aren't failures.
  They're the topics most likely to come up as hard questions, so your plan
  sends you to them first. Each gap has a **Practise** button.

**See the full match** opens every requirement, with why it matters and what
the interviewer is likely to ask.

## Will it get shortlisted?

Under your resume on the **Resume match** tab, **Will it get shortlisted?**
checks what screening software (ATS) and a recruiter's quick scan look for:

- **Job keywords:** how many of the job's skills and keywords your resume
  uses, and which are missing.
- **Main skills near the top:** whether your matching skills appear in the
  top third, where recruiters look first.
- **The job's title:** whether your resume uses the same title.
- **Standard section headings:** Experience, Education and Skills.
- **Contact details:** email and phone as plain text.
- **Results with numbers:** how many bullets show a measurable result.
- **Length:** about 1–2 pages.
- **Readable by screening software:** no scanned PDF, tables or columns.

Each item that needs work says how to fix it, and **Tailor my resume** helps
with missing keywords. It runs in your browser on your resume (or this job's
tailored copy) and uses no credits. Every company's tools differ; these are
the common basics. Only add skills you really have.

**How shortlisting works** (open it under the checks) explains the four usual
steps: screening software filters, a recruiter's quick scan, the hiring
manager's close read, and who gets invited, with what helps at each one.

## Skills this job wants

Also on **Resume match**, **Skills this job wants** lists the skills the job
asks for and ticks the ones your resume already shows. Open a missing skill
to see how to get it:

- **Learn:** the study library page for it, and the official docs.
- **Build:** a small project that proves it.
- **Prove it:** a resume line to adapt once it's true, with your real numbers.
- **Optional certification**, where a common one exists.

**Ask the AI Coach** opens the coach with that skill, and **How to succeed**
has five short tips. It uses no credits.

## Tailor your resume

On the **Resume** tab choose **Tailor my resume for this job**. OfferReady
rewrites your bullets for this job's skills and gaps **without inventing
anything**.

1. Review each bullet. For each one choose **Accept** or **Keep original**.
   You can edit a rewrite before you accept it.
2. A bullet marked **Needs a fact from you** has a gap in [brackets]. Fill in
   your real number or detail first.
3. **Put them in your resume:**
   - **Save tailored copy** saves a copy of your resume with the accepted
     bullets, **with this job only**. Your main resume doesn't change, so your
     other jobs keep using it. Or:
   - **Copy into your resume file** and paste over the old bullets in Word or
     Google Docs.
4. **Next: re-check my fit** runs the match again with the tailored copy, so
   you can see the difference.

**Undo my edits** on a bullet puts back the AI's suggestion before you edited it.

### Where the tailored copy is

On the job's **Resume match** tab, under **Tailored for this job**:

- **Show the tailored resume**, **Copy the text** or **Download .txt**.
- **Tailor again** to change it.
- **Use my main resume instead** deletes the copy (it asks first). The job
  goes back to your main resume.

The tailored copy is kept on this device, like your main resume.

## Your plan

The plan is a list of tasks for each day up to your interview date. It puts
your weakest areas first, spaces out reviews, puts mock interviews halfway and
near the end, and keeps the day before light.

- **Your plan for today** is on the job page. Tick tasks as you finish them.
- Missed a day? **Catch up from earlier days** shows what's left.
- **Add to calendar** downloads the plan as calendar events (with a pass).
- Change the interview date and the plan fits the days you have.

## Common questions

??? question "The job title is wrong"
    Choose **Rename** next to the title on the job page, or change it on the
    **Settings** tab and choose **Save changes**. Imported jobs use the
    exact title from the posting.

??? question "Can I edit the job description after adding the job?"
    On **Questions for this job**, open **Edit the job description first**
    before writing your questions. To re-analyze a very different description,
    add it as a new job.

??? question "Why is a skill I have listed as a gap?"
    Your resume doesn't show it clearly enough for the match to find it. Add a
    bullet that names the skill and a result, or use **Tailor my resume**.
    Then re-check the match.

??? question "Does tailoring change my resume for my other jobs?"
    No. Tailoring saves a separate copy with the job you tailored it for. Your
    main resume, and every other job, stay as they were.

??? question "Does tailoring make things up?"
    No. It only rewords what's already in your resume. Anything it would need
    from you, like a number, is left in [brackets] and marked
    **Needs a fact from you**.

??? question "Does deleting a job delete my stories?"
    No. Stories belong to you, not to one job. Deleting a job removes its
    analysis, plan and practice for that job.

??? question "My interview date has passed"
    The job page asks how it went. Log the round, set a new date if it was
    rescheduled, or record the outcome. See
    [After the interview](after-the-interview.md).
