# Where to start

The library is big. You don't need all of it. Answer two questions below, then
read the pages **in the order shown**. Each page also has **Next** at the bottom.

## 1. How much time do you have?

- **Interview in the next 2 weeks:** open your role guide below and go
  straight to its **14-day plan**. Then the
  [Master Cheat Sheets](../Personal-SourceCode/Interview_Cheat_Sheets.md), and
  practise your real job in the [OfferReady app](https://getofferready.app/).
- **Interview in about a month:** read your whole role guide below, then follow
  the [30-Day Prep Plan](../Personal-SourceCode/Interview_30_Day_Plan.md).
- **No interview yet, learning:** read the core path for your role below, one
  page a day. Then build something small with a [Setup Guide](../Setup-Guides/index.md).
- **New to GenAI completely:** start with [New to GenAI](#new-to-genai) below,
  then pick a role.

## 2. Which role?

Each role has a guide: what the role is, the interview loop, what interviewers
score, the likely questions and a 14-day plan. Read the guide first, then the
core pages in order.

## New to GenAI

Read these in order. Each builds on the one before.

1. [LLM Fundamentals](../GenAI-Topics/llm-fundamentals/index.md): what a model is, tokens, context, temperature
2. [Prompt Engineering](../GenAI-Topics/prompt-engineering/index.md): getting reliable output
3. [Embedding Models](../GenAI-Topics/embeddings/index.md) and [Vector DB](../GenAI-Topics/vector-db/index.md): search by meaning
4. [RAG](../GenAI-Topics/rag/index.md): answering from your own documents
5. [Agent Engineering](../GenAI-Topics/agent-engineering/index.md): models that use tools
6. [First RAG App](../Setup-Guides/first-rag/index.md): build one yourself

Then pick your role above.
